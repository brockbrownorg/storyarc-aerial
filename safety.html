Safety and Rules

Can we fly at your venue? Let us check.

That is the first question every Omaha buyer asks, and it deserves a straight answer before any money moves. Great footage is never worth an unsafe or unlawful flight. Here is how we answer it, and how your guests, your neighbors, and your reputation stay protected.

Your questions, answered straight

Rules vary by address. Downtown near the airport is not the same sky as a farm field outside Gretna. We sort out which ones apply to your project during planning, not on the day.

Airspace First

Before a booking is confirmed, we check the airspace over your location. In Omaha that can mean controlled airspace around Eppley Airfield, or venue permission at places like the RiverFront parks, and we handle both routes during planning. Where an authorization is required, we secure it before flight day. If the airspace says no, we tell you early and design around it.

Who Handles the Permissions?

Property permission, venue rules, and event organizer approvals are confirmed before we fly. We flag whatever a location will not allow while there is still time to plan around it.

What About My Guests?

Flight paths are drawn to keep clear of crowds and uninvolved people, with a visual observer on complex flights. A great shot is never worth putting someone at risk.

What If the Weather Turns?

Wind and weather are checked before the flight and watched through the day. When conditions fall apart, we reschedule the aerial work instead of forcing it, and we make the call early enough for you to plan around it.

What About the Neighbors?

We film the subject you hired us for, and only that subject. No lingering over neighboring private spaces, and no shots of people who did not agree to be filmed.

A No, Early, With an Alternative

If a requested shot breaks a rule or crosses a safety line, we say no early and plainly, and offer an alternative that still serves your story. That honesty protects your event and your name, and it is part of what you hire us for.

Planning a shoot somewhere unusual?

Airports, stadiums, historic sites, and crowded events each carry their own limits. Ask before you book, and we will work out what is possible while the plan is still flexible.
